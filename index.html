What this is

Several trading firms screen candidates with a fast, no-calculator arithmetic test before any interview. This does not reproduce any firm's assessment — the widely circulated 80 questions in 8 minutes format turns out to have no firm-published source at any of the six firms checked. What it reproduces is the constraint: far more questions than there is time to answer carefully. The sourced findings are in RESEARCH.md.
